Why Japan works so well from Los Angeles

LAX is the best U.S. gateway to Japan. The nonstop flight to Tokyo on JAL or ANA runs about 11 hours — several hours shorter than from New York or Miami. You leave at night, sleep on the plane, and land in Tokyo with the day ahead of you.

For the Mexican and Central American community of Southern California, Japan is no longer a far-off trip. It is a one-week circuit with a Spanish-speaking guide the whole way and no language barrier at any point.

What the tour includes

  • Central hotels in Tokyo and Osaka with daily breakfast
  • A Spanish-speaking guide for the entire route
  • Bullet train (Shinkansen) Tokyo–Osaka with a reserved seat
  • Sightseeing: Asakusa and Senso-ji temple, the Shibuya crossing, Osaka Castle and Dotonbori
  • Airport transfers and on-trip assistance
  • Optional excursions to Kyoto, Nara and Mount Fuji with a Spanish-speaking guide

The international flight from LAX is quoted separately so we can find you the best fare on your dates. Meals not listed and travel insurance are not included; insurance can be arranged with us.

Documents for Hispanic travelers in California

Citizens of Mexico, Argentina, Chile, Costa Rica and most South American countries do not need a visa to enter Japan as tourists for up to 90 days. Neither does a U.S. passport. If your passport requires a Japanese visa, we tell you the process and prepare the supporting paperwork. See our guide to traveling on a Latin American passport while living in the U.S.
